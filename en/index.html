A recent post from the community

Environment versus willpower.Get the candy off the table.

I’m probably the biggest advocate of the idea that it’s the environment that shapes your future. A lot has already been said about this from all kinds of scientific angles. But today I was intrigued by a case from Chile.

A fairly recent one: a national experiment was carried out in 2018 (opens in a new tab).

Any products with too much sugar, salt or fat got a black octagon on the package. Plus a ban on advertising.

As a result, in just three years sugar purchases fell by 37%, and the total calories in the average shopping basket dropped by 23%.

People started counting the labels in their heads: if a package had one octagon, they still bought it. Big deal, just a few calories. But with two — noticeably less often, with three — even less often, and with four — almost never.

At the same time, people didn’t start spending less money. They bought unhealthy products noticeably less often, and the healthy ones that replaced them — more often.

That’s simply how we’re built. If there’s no candy at home, sooner or later you’ll start eating fruit instead, get used to it and make it part of yourself.

No TikTok? You’ll open YouTube Shorts. And if that’s not there either — you’ll take a book off the shelf.

Consumption doesn’t go down, but it shifts toward what’s available and seems more worthwhile, less costly for the mind.

Of course, you shouldn’t count on officials to set up your environment for the sake of your health and productivity. Instead, create it yourself. At least where you can reach.
